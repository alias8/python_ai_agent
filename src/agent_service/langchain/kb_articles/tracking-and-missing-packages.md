# Tracking and missing packages

When your order ships, we email you a tracking link. You can also find tracking details at any time under **Order history** by selecting the order. If your order ships in more than one package, each package has its own tracking link.

## Understanding tracking statuses

- **Label created:** we've printed the shipping label, but the carrier hasn't picked up the package yet. It's normal for this status to last up to 24 hours.
- **In transit:** the carrier has the package and it's moving through their network.
- **Out for delivery:** the package is on a delivery vehicle and should arrive today.
- **Delivered:** the carrier has recorded the package as delivered.
- **Exception:** something is delaying the package, such as bad weather, an incorrect address, or a missed delivery attempt. The carrier usually retries automatically.

## If tracking hasn't updated

Tracking can pause for a day or two, especially over weekends or while a package moves between facilities. If your tracking hasn't changed for 5 business days, contact us and we'll ask the carrier to trace the package.

## If your package is late

Delivery estimates aren't guaranteed, but if your package hasn't arrived 3 business days after the latest estimated delivery date, contact us. If you paid for expedited or next day shipping and your package arrived late because of a carrier delay, we'll refund the shipping charge — just ask.

## If tracking says delivered but you don't have it

Packages sometimes show as delivered a little before they actually arrive, or are left somewhere unexpected. Before contacting us, please:

1. Wait 48 hours after the delivered scan. Most "missing" packages turn up within that time.
2. Check around your property, including side doors, garages, and mailrooms, and ask neighbors or your building manager.
3. Confirm the shipping address on the order is correct.

If the package still hasn't turned up after 48 hours, contact us within 30 days of the delivered date. We'll file a claim with the carrier, which takes up to 5 business days to investigate. Once the investigation is complete, we'll send a free replacement or issue a full refund, whichever you prefer. For orders over $500, we may ask you to sign a short statement confirming you didn't receive the package before we replace it.

## Returned to sender

If the carrier can't deliver a package — for example, because the address is incomplete — it's returned to us. Once it arrives, we'll refund you automatically, minus the original shipping cost. We can't reship packages returned to sender; please place a new order with the corrected address.
