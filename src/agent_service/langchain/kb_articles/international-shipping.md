# International shipping

We ship to more than 40 countries, including Canada, the United Kingdom, most of the European Union, Australia, New Zealand, and Japan. The full list of countries appears in the country dropdown at checkout; if your country isn't listed, we can't ship there yet.

## Delivery times and costs

International orders ship via our international courier partner and usually arrive within 6-12 business days. Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on the destination, weight, and size of your order. Free shipping and Plus shipping benefits apply only to US addresses.

## Duties, taxes, and customs

For Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union, and Australia, duties and import taxes are calculated at checkout and included in your order total. You won't be charged anything extra on delivery.

For all other countries, orders ship "delivered duty unpaid." This means your local customs authority may charge you duties, taxes, and a brokerage fee before releasing the package. These charges are set by your country, not by us, and we can't estimate them in advance. If you refuse to pay them, the package is returned to us and we refund the order minus the original shipping cost.

## Tracking international orders

You'll receive a tracking number when your order ships. Tracking may pause for several days while the package clears customs; this is normal. If tracking hasn't updated for more than 10 business days, contact us and we'll open an investigation with the courier.

## Items we can't ship internationally

Some items can't be shipped outside the US because of carrier or customs restrictions:

- Products containing lithium batteries, including laptops, power banks, and cordless power tools.
- Aerosols, fuels, and other flammable or pressurized items.
- Knives with blades longer than 3 inches, to most destinations.
- Oversized items that ship by freight.

If your cart contains a restricted item, checkout shows a message and removes it before you pay.

## Returning an international order

International orders can be returned within 30 days of delivery, like domestic orders. However, we can't provide a prepaid return label for international addresses: you'll need to ship the item back at your own cost, using a tracked service. Once we receive and inspect it, we refund the item price. Original shipping costs, duties, and import taxes are not refundable, because we can't recover them from customs authorities.
