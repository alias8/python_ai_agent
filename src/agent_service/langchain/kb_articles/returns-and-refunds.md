# Returns and refunds

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to your original payment method. The 30 days start on the date your carrier marks the package as delivered, not the date you placed the order.

## How to start a return

1. Sign in and open **Order history**.
2. Select the order, then choose **Return items**.
3. Pick the items you want to send back and tell us why. The reason doesn't change whether the return is accepted, but it helps us fix problems with products and listings.
4. Print the prepaid return label, or choose the QR code option and have a drop-off location print it for you.

Pack the items securely, ideally in the original packaging, and drop them off within 7 days of creating the return. Labels that aren't used within 7 days expire, and you'll need to start a new return.

## When you'll get your money

We inspect returned items within 2 business days of receiving them. Once the inspection passes, we issue the refund straight away. Card refunds usually take 5-10 business days to appear, depending on your bank. Refunds to store credit or a gift card are instant.

Original shipping charges are refunded only if the return is because of our mistake: a wrong, damaged, or defective item. For all other returns, the prepaid label costs $6.95, which is deducted from your refund. Plus members get free return labels.

## Items that can't be returned

Some items are excluded from standard returns:

- **Final sale items.** Anything marked "Final Sale" on the product page, including clearance items with a price ending in .97, can't be returned or exchanged.
- **Personal care products** that have been opened, such as razors and hair tools, for hygiene reasons.
- **Digital products and gift cards.**
- **Custom or personalized items**, such as engraved products, unless they arrived damaged or with our error.

## Opened electronics

Electronics can be returned within the same 30-day window. However, electronics that have been opened and are not defective are subject to a 15% restocking fee, deducted from your refund. To avoid the fee, return electronics unopened with all seals intact. If an electronic item is faulty, see our article on damaged or defective items instead — defective items are never charged a restocking fee.

## Returns without a receipt

If you received an item as a gift, you can return it for store credit using the gift receipt or the order number. The refund goes to a gift card in your name rather than to the buyer's payment method, so the buyer isn't notified.
