# Canceling or changing an order

We start preparing orders quickly so they ship on time, which means there's a short window for canceling or changing them.

## Canceling an order

You can cancel an order yourself within 60 minutes of placing it:

1. Go to **Order history** and select the order.
2. Choose **Cancel order**.
3. Select the items you want to cancel, or cancel the whole order.

If the **Cancel order** button isn't shown, the order has already moved to "Processing," which means our warehouse has started picking and packing it. Once an order is processing, we can't cancel it. You can refuse the delivery when it arrives, or return the items once they're delivered.

When you cancel, we release the authorization hold on your card straight away. Your bank decides when the pending charge disappears, which usually takes 3-5 business days.

## Canceling part of an order

You can cancel individual items while the order is still within the 60-minute window. If canceling an item takes your order total below $50, the free standard shipping no longer applies and the $5.95 shipping charge is added to the remaining items.

## Changing items or quantities

We can't add items to an order or change quantities after it's placed. To add an item, place a separate order. To change an item, cancel it within 60 minutes and place a new order with the item you want.

## Changing the shipping address

You can change the shipping address from **Order history** until the shipping label is created, which is usually within a few hours of ordering. After the label is created, the address can't be changed by us. You may be able to redirect the package using the carrier's own delivery management service once it's in transit, but this isn't guaranteed and any fee charged by the carrier is your responsibility.

For fraud prevention, orders paid with a new card can only be changed to an address that matches the card's billing address.

## Pre-orders and backordered items

Pre-orders and items marked "Ships in 2-3 weeks" can be canceled at any time until they ship, not just in the first 60 minutes. Your card isn't charged for these items until they ship.

## Orders placed by mistake

If you placed an order twice by accident, cancel the duplicate within 60 minutes. If the window has passed, refuse the second delivery or return it; we'll waive the return shipping fee for accidental duplicate orders if you contact us first.
