# Price adjustments

If the price of an item you bought from us drops within 14 days of your purchase, we'll refund you the difference. You don't need to return the item.

## How to request a price adjustment

1. Go to **Order history** and select the order.
2. Choose **Request price adjustment** next to the item.
3. We check the current price automatically and, if it's lower, refund the difference to your original payment method within 2 business days.

You can also contact us and we'll do it for you. Requests must be made within 14 days of the order date, not the delivery date.

## What counts as a lower price

The price must be lower on our own website, for the same item, in the same size and color, and it must be available to buy when you make the request. The adjustment is based on the item's selling price, and doesn't include shipping costs or taxes, except that the tax you paid on the difference is also refunded.

Each item can get one price adjustment. If the price drops again after we've adjusted it, we can't adjust it a second time.

## Price adjustments and promo codes

If you used a promo code on your original order, the adjustment is calculated on the price you actually paid after the discount. We can't apply a new promo code to a past order.

## What's not eligible

Some price changes aren't eligible for adjustments:

- **Limited-time events:** doorbuster deals, flash sales lasting less than 24 hours, and Black Friday and Cyber Monday prices.
- **Clearance and final sale prices.**
- **Plus early access pricing,** when the item was bought by someone who isn't a Plus member.
- **Bundle prices,** when the lower price is only available when buying items together.
- **Other retailers' prices.** We don't match competitors' prices, including marketplace sellers on other websites.
- **Items bought with Pay in 4,** until the installment plan has been fully paid.

## Price increases

If an item's price goes up after you buy it, you won't be charged the difference.

## Returning instead

If the price drops more than 14 days after you bought the item, but you're still within the return window, you can return the original item and buy it again at the new price. Return shipping fees apply to standard returns.
