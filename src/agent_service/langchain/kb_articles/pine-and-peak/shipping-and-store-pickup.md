# Shipping and store pickup

We ship to all 50 US states. We don't currently ship internationally or to US territories.

## Shipping costs and times

Shipping is a flat $8 per order, and free on orders over $100. Orders ship within 2 business days from our warehouse and usually arrive 2-6 business days after that, depending on distance. We use a single ground shipping service for every order; there's no expedited option.

You'll get a tracking link by email once your order ships. Orders containing items from different warehouses may ship separately, each with its own tracking link, at no extra cost.

## Free in-store pickup

Choose **Pick up in store** at checkout to collect your order from any of our stores for free. We'll email you when it's ready, usually within 4 hours for in-stock items. Bring your order confirmation and photo ID. Orders are held for 7 days; after that they're canceled and refunded.

Someone else can collect your order if you add their name at checkout. They'll need their own photo ID.

## Items with shipping restrictions

Fuel canisters, bear spray, and other hazardous materials can't be shipped at all; they're available for in-store pickup only. Kayaks, canoes, and other boats over 8 feet long ship by freight, which takes 7-10 business days and costs a flat $150, even on orders over $100.

## PO boxes

We can ship most items to PO boxes, but not freight items or orders over $1,000, which need a street address and a signature on delivery.

## Delivery problems

If your tracking shows delivered but you can't find your package, wait one business day and check with neighbors, then contact us. We'll open a claim with the carrier and ship a replacement once the claim is filed; you won't have to wait for the carrier to finish investigating.
