# Returns and exchanges

Unused gear can be returned within 14 days of delivery or in-store pickup for a full refund to your original payment method. We keep our return window short because much of what we sell is seasonal, but we're flexible about why you're returning something.

## Starting a return

Returns are free. Sign in, open **My orders**, and choose **Return or exchange** next to the item. We'll email you a prepaid label. You can also bring the item to any of our stores with your order number; in-store returns are refunded on the spot.

Items must be unused, with tags attached and in the original packaging. Boots and shoes should be tried on indoors only — footwear with dirt or wear on the soles can't be returned as unused.

## Used gear

If you've taken gear out on the trail and it isn't working for you, we'll still take it back within 14 days, but as store credit rather than a refund. Used gear is inspected, cleaned, and resold through our used gear program. Store credit never expires.

## Exchanges

Exchanges for a different size or color follow the same 14-day window and are always free, including the shipping on the replacement. If you'd like a different product entirely, return the original and place a new order.

## When refunds arrive

Mail-in returns are refunded within 3 business days of reaching our warehouse. Your bank may take a further 3-7 business days to show the credit. Store credit is issued as soon as the return is inspected.

## What can't be returned

- Fuel canisters and other hazardous materials, once they've left our store or warehouse.
- Rental gear — see our article on gear rentals for how rentals are returned.
- Custom-fitted items, such as heat-molded boot liners, once they've been fitted.
- Gift cards.

## Defective items

If an item fails because of a manufacturing defect, it isn't covered by this 14-day window — it's covered by our repair program and warranty instead. See our article on repairs and warranties.
