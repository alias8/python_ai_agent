# Gear rentals

Not ready to buy? You can rent tents, sleeping bags, backpacks, snowshoes, and climbing shoes from any of our stores, by the day or by the week.

## How renting works

Reserve rental gear online up to 60 days in advance, then collect it from the store you chose. Rentals start the day you collect them and are due back by closing time on the last day. A weekend rental — collected Friday, returned Monday — is charged as two days.

Rental gear can only be collected and returned in person, at the same store. We can't ship rentals.

## Prices and deposit

Rental prices are listed on each item's page. When you collect your gear, we place a $50 refundable deposit on your card for each item. The deposit is released when the gear comes back in good condition, usually within 2 business days.

If you decide to keep a rented item, you can buy it within 7 days of returning it, and we'll deduct the full rental fee from the purchase price.

## Late returns

Late returns are charged $10 per item for each extra day, up to the item's full replacement price. If you know you'll be late, call the store — we can usually extend a rental at the normal daily rate if the gear isn't reserved by someone else.

## Cleaning and damage

Please return gear dry and free of food. We'll clean it; a $15 cleaning fee applies if tents come back wet or muddy enough to need a full wash. Normal wear is expected and isn't charged. Damage beyond normal wear, such as a torn tent floor or a broken backpack frame, is charged at the repair cost, deducted from your deposit first. Lost gear is charged at the replacement price.

## Canceling a reservation

Cancel a rental reservation for free up to 48 hours before collection. Later cancellations are charged one day's rental fee.
