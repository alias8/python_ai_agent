# Your account and password

You need an account to rent gear, request repairs, and see your order history. Accounts are free.

## Resetting your password

1. On the sign-in page, select **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Select the link in the email we send you and choose a new password of at least 12 characters.

Password reset links expire after 60 minutes. If yours has expired, request a new one. If the email doesn't arrive within 10 minutes, check your spam folder.

## Account lockout

After 10 failed sign-in attempts, your account is locked for one hour to protect it. You can unlock it straight away by resetting your password.

## Signing in with a store account

If you've only ever shopped with us in store, you may already have an account: store receipts are linked to your phone number. Select **Find my in-store account** on the sign-in page and we'll text you a code to claim it.

## Changing your email address

You can change your email address under **Account settings**. We'll send a confirmation link to the new address, and a notification to the old one. The change only takes effect once you select the confirmation link.

## Closing your account

Contact us to close your account. Before you do, return any rental gear, collect any items in for repair, and use your store credit, which is lost when the account is closed. We delete your account within 30 days, but keep order records for 7 years because the law requires it.
