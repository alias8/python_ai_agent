# Payments and gift cards

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover, as well as our gift cards and store credit. We don't offer installment plans or buy-now-pay-later options, and we don't accept checks, cash on delivery, or cryptocurrency.

Your card is charged when your order ships, or when you collect an in-store pickup order.

## Payment error messages

If your payment doesn't go through, you'll see a message with a code starting with "P-":

- **P-201: Card declined.** Your bank declined the payment. Contact your bank or try another card.
- **P-202: Billing address mismatch.** The billing address doesn't match what your bank has on file. Check the street number and ZIP code.
- **P-305: Payment system busy.** Our payment system didn't respond. Wait 5 minutes and check your email for an order confirmation before trying again, so you don't order twice.

## Gift cards

Gift cards come in any amount from $25 to $250 and are available as e-gift cards or as physical cards from our stores. Gift cards never expire and have no fees. You can use a gift card online or in any store, and combine it with a credit card for the remaining balance.

Gift cards can't be redeemed for cash, except where the law requires it, and can't be used to pay rental deposits.

## Store credit

Store credit from returns of used gear works like a gift card and never expires. It's linked to your account rather than a card number, and applied automatically at checkout unless you choose not to use it.

## Member discount

Members of our free membership program get 10% off full-price items once a year, during their birthday month. The discount can't be combined with other promotions and doesn't apply to rentals, gift cards, or sale items.
