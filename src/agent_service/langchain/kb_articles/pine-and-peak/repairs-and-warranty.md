# Repairs and warranty

We'd rather fix your gear than replace it. Everything we sell under our own brand comes with free lifetime repairs, and every product is covered against manufacturing defects.

## Free lifetime repairs on our own brand

If anything from our own brand needs repairing — a broken zipper, a torn seam, a snapped tent pole — we'll repair it for free, for as long as you own it, whether the damage came from a defect or from use. You don't need a receipt. Repairs usually take 2-3 weeks.

To start a repair, bring the item to any store or request a free mail-in repair label online. You'll get a repair ticket number starting with "RPR-", which you can use to check the repair's status on our repairs page.

## Other brands

Products from other brands are covered by their manufacturer's warranty. We'll handle the warranty claim for you if you bring the item to a store within one year of purchase. After a year, contact the manufacturer directly.

## Defects

Our warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship for the life of the product. If we can't repair a defective item, we'll replace it or give you store credit for its current price. Warranty claims don't cover normal wear, such as worn-out boot soles or faded fabric, or damage from misuse — although our own-brand items can still be repaired for free.

## Repair status messages

- **RPR received:** your item has arrived at our repair center.
- **RPR in progress:** we're working on it.
- **RPR waiting on parts:** a replacement part has been ordered; this can add up to 2 weeks.
- **RPR ready:** your item has been repaired and is being shipped back, or is ready to collect.

## Items we can't repair

We can't repair items with damage from fire or chemical exposure, or electronics such as headlamps and GPS units — electronics are replaced under warranty instead.
