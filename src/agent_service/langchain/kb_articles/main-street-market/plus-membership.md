# Plus membership

Plus is our paid membership program. It costs $49 per year, or $5.99 per month, and includes:

- Free expedited (1-2 business day) shipping on all eligible orders, with no minimum.
- Free return labels on all returns.
- An extended 60-day return window, instead of the standard 30 days.
- Early access to seasonal sales, 24 hours before everyone else.
- A 5% reward on every purchase, paid as store credit each quarter.

## Joining Plus

You can join Plus from **Account settings** or at checkout. Your benefits start as soon as you join, so you can use free expedited shipping on the order you're placing. New members can try Plus free for 30 days; if you don't cancel before the trial ends, you're charged for the plan you chose.

## Sharing Plus

Plus benefits apply to one account. You can't share your membership with other household members, but a gift membership can be bought for someone else from the Plus page.

## How the extended return window works

The 60-day return window applies to items bought while your membership is active. If your membership ends, items you already bought keep the 60-day window. The extended window doesn't apply to final sale items, and opened electronics are still subject to the restocking fee.

## Rewards

Your 5% reward is calculated on the item total, excluding tax, shipping, and gift cards. Rewards are paid as store credit at the start of each quarter and expire 12 months after they're issued. If you return an item, the reward earned on it is removed.

## Canceling Plus

You can cancel at any time from **Account settings**, then **Plus membership**. What happens next depends on when you cancel:

- **During the free trial:** you won't be charged.
- **Within 30 days of a paid annual renewal:** if you haven't used any Plus benefits since the renewal, we'll refund the full $49. If you have used benefits, the membership stays active until the end of the year and isn't refunded.
- **After 30 days:** annual memberships aren't refundable, but your benefits continue until the end of the paid year and the membership won't renew.
- **Monthly plans:** benefits continue until the end of the current month.

## Exclusions

Plus shipping benefits apply to US addresses only, and don't cover freight items, next day shipping, or international orders. Unused rewards are lost if your membership is canceled because of a chargeback.
