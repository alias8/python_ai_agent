# Pending and duplicate charges

Seeing a charge you don't expect on your bank statement can be worrying. In most cases, it's a temporary authorization hold rather than a real charge, and it disappears on its own.

## Authorization holds

When you place an order, we ask your bank to reserve the order total on your card. This is called an authorization hold, and it shows up in your banking app as a pending transaction. The money isn't actually taken until your order ships. When it ships, the pending transaction is replaced by the final charge.

If an order is canceled before it ships, or a payment attempt fails, we release the hold straight away. However, it's your bank that decides when the pending transaction disappears from your account, and this usually takes 3-5 business days. Some banks take up to 10 business days. We can't speed this up, but your bank may be able to release it sooner if you contact them.

## Why the final charge might be different

The final charge can be lower than the pending amount if part of your order was canceled or was out of stock. It's never higher than the amount you authorized. If items ship separately, the single pending hold is replaced by one charge per package.

## Two charges for one order

If you see two charges for one order, check whether they're both pending or both posted:

- **One pending and one posted:** this is usually the original authorization hold plus the final charge. The pending one disappears within 3-5 business days.
- **Two pending charges:** this often happens when a payment was retried — for example after an error at checkout. Only one becomes a real charge; the other hold is released.
- **Two posted charges for the same amount:** this may be a genuine duplicate charge. Check **Order history** first to make sure you didn't place two orders. If there's only one order, contact us and we'll refund the duplicate within 2 business days.

## Charges you don't recognize

If you see a charge from us that doesn't match any order in your account, check whether another household member has an account or used your card. If you still don't recognize it, contact us and your bank. Don't file a chargeback with your bank before contacting us: a chargeback freezes the order while the bank investigates, which can take up to 90 days, and we can't issue a refund while the chargeback is open. Contacting us first is almost always faster.
