# Shipping options and delivery times

We offer three shipping speeds for addresses in the contiguous United States. Delivery times are counted in business days, from the day your order ships, and don't include weekends or public holidays.

| Option | Delivery time | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 3-5 business days | $5.95, free on orders over $50 |
| Expedited | 1-2 business days | $14.95, free for Plus members |
| Next day | Next business day | $24.95 |

## When your order ships

Orders placed before 2pm Eastern Time on a business day usually ship the same day. Orders placed after 2pm, or on a weekend or holiday, ship the next business day. If your order contains items shipping from different warehouses, it may arrive in more than one package, each with its own tracking number. You're only charged shipping once.

Items shown as "Ships in 2-3 weeks" or "Pre-order" on the product page ship separately when they become available. The delivery estimate at checkout already includes that wait.

## Alaska, Hawaii, and US territories

Orders to Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, and other US territories ship via standard shipping only and take 5-10 business days. The free shipping threshold still applies.

## Restrictions on faster shipping

Expedited and next day shipping are not available for PO boxes or for military APO/FPO/DPO addresses; orders to these addresses always ship via standard postal service. Next day shipping is also not available for orders containing hazardous materials, such as aerosols, lithium batteries sold separately, or flammable liquids, because these must travel by ground.

## Large and oversized items

Furniture, large appliances, and other oversized items ship by freight rather than parcel carrier. Freight delivery takes 7-14 business days. Once your item reaches the local freight terminal, the carrier calls or texts you to schedule a delivery window. Freight delivery is to the ground floor or garage only; in-home placement and packaging removal ("white glove") is available at checkout for an extra $79. Freight items aren't eligible for free shipping or for Plus shipping benefits, and a flat $99 freight fee applies per order.
