# Payment error codes

If your payment doesn't go through at checkout, you'll see an error message with a code starting with "E-". This article explains what each code means and what to do next. Your order isn't placed until payment succeeds, so you won't be charged for a failed attempt — although some banks briefly show a pending authorization, which disappears on its own.

## E-4012: Card declined by your bank

Your bank declined the transaction. We aren't told the reason, but common causes are insufficient funds, a daily spending limit, or your bank's fraud checks flagging an unusual purchase. Contact your bank, or try a different card. Retrying the same card repeatedly usually won't help and may cause your bank to block the card temporarily.

## E-4020: Address verification failed

The billing address you entered doesn't match the address your bank has on file for the card. Check the street number and ZIP code carefully — these are the parts banks compare. If you've moved recently, your bank may still have your old address. Prepaid cards without a registered address always fail this check.

## E-4031: Card authentication failed

Your bank asked you to confirm the payment, for example with a code sent by text message or through your banking app, and the confirmation wasn't completed. This can happen if the confirmation window was closed, timed out, or blocked by a pop-up blocker. Try again and complete the confirmation step within 5 minutes. If you don't receive the confirmation request, your bank's app may need updating.

## E-4045: Card expired or details incorrect

The card number, expiration date, or security code doesn't match. Check the details and try again, or update the saved card under **Payment methods**.

## E-5003: Payment processor timeout

Our payment processor didn't respond in time. This is a problem on our side, not with your card. **Don't retry immediately.** In rare cases, the payment actually succeeded even though you saw this error, and retrying can place a second order. Wait 10 minutes, then check **Order history** and your email for an order confirmation. If there's no confirmation after 10 minutes, it's safe to try again. If you see two pending charges on your card after an E-5003 error, one of them is an authorization hold that will be released automatically — see our article on pending and duplicate charges.

## E-5010: Payment service unavailable

Payments are temporarily unavailable, usually during scheduled maintenance. Please try again in 30 minutes. Your cart is saved.
