# Privacy and data requests

You can see, download, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. This article explains how.

## What information we hold

We hold the information you give us, such as your name, email address, shipping and billing addresses, phone number, and order history. We also collect information about how you use our website and app, such as pages viewed and items saved. We don't store your full card numbers; our payment processor does.

## Downloading your data

Go to **Account settings**, then **Privacy**, and select **Download my data**. We'll email you a link to a file containing your account information, order history, saved addresses, and marketing preferences within 7 days. The link is valid for 14 days.

## Correcting your information

You can update your name, email address, phone number, and saved addresses at any time in **Account settings**. You can't change the details on orders that have already shipped.

## Marketing emails and texts

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link at the bottom of any email, and from marketing texts by replying STOP. You can also manage all your preferences under **Privacy**. Unsubscribing from marketing doesn't stop order confirmations, shipping updates, and security emails, which we need to send you.

## Deleting your account

Go to **Account settings**, then **Privacy**, and select **Delete my account**. Before you delete your account:

- Use or transfer any store credit and rewards; these are lost when the account is deleted.
- Cancel your Plus membership, if you have one.
- Wait for any open orders, returns, or refunds to finish.

We confirm your request by email, and complete the deletion within 30 days. Deletion can't be undone. Once it's complete, you can't sign in, and your order history, saved addresses, and wishlists are gone.

## Information we keep after deletion

Some information has to be kept even after your account is deleted, because the law requires it. We keep records of your orders and payments for 7 years for tax and accounting purposes, and we keep records needed to prevent fraud or resolve open disputes, such as chargebacks. This information is stored separately, isn't used for marketing, and is deleted when the retention period ends.

## Requests from other regions

Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights. We handle all requests the same way regardless of location, and don't charge a fee for them. For requests you can't make from your account, contact our privacy team through the help center.
