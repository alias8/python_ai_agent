# Payment methods

We accept the following payment methods for online orders:

- Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover credit and debit cards.
- Digital wallets available in your browser or device at checkout.
- Our store gift cards and store credit.
- Pay in 4 installments, for eligible orders.

Your card is authorized when you place your order, but the charge isn't finalized until your order ships. If an order ships in more than one package, you may see a separate charge for each package.

## Saving a card

You can save cards to your account under **Account settings**, then **Payment methods**, to check out faster next time. We store card details with our payment processor, not on our own servers, and we only ever show the last four digits of a saved card. You can remove a saved card at any time; cards linked to an active subscription or pending order can't be removed until that order ships.

## Splitting payment

You can pay with a gift card or store credit and put the remaining balance on a card. You can't split an order across two different credit cards.

## Paying in 4 installments

For orders between $100 and $3,000, you can choose **Pay in 4** at checkout. You pay 25% of the order total up front, then three more payments every two weeks, with no interest or fees if you pay on time. Approval depends on a soft credit check, which doesn't affect your credit score. Installment plans are provided by our financing partner, and questions about payment schedules are handled by them rather than by us. If you return items from an order paid in installments, the refund reduces your remaining payments first.

## Billing address

The billing address you enter at checkout must match the address your bank has on file for the card. If it doesn't, your payment may be declined with an address verification error. See our article on payment error codes for details.

## Payment methods we don't accept

We don't accept personal checks, money orders, wire transfers, cryptocurrency, or cash on delivery. We also can't accept prepaid cards that don't have a registered billing address, because they fail address verification. Purchase orders and invoicing are only available for approved business accounts.

## Currency

All prices on our site are in US dollars. If you pay with a card issued outside the US, your bank converts the charge into your currency and may add a foreign transaction fee. We don't control these fees.
