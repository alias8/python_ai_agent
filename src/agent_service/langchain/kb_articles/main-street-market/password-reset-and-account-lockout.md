# Password reset and account lockout

If you've forgotten your password, you can reset it yourself in a couple of minutes.

## Resetting your password

1. On the sign-in page, select **Forgot password?**
2. Enter the email address for your account.
3. Open the email we send you and select **Reset password**.
4. Choose a new password. It must be at least 10 characters long and can't be one you've used for this account before.

If you don't receive the email within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder, and make sure you entered the email address you used to create the account. For security, we show the same message whether or not an account exists for the address you entered, so we can't confirm which email address your account uses.

## If you no longer have access to your email

If you can't access the email address on your account anymore — for example, an old work email — contact support. We'll verify your identity by asking for the last four digits of a card used on a recent order and the order number, and then update the email address for you. For security, we can't change the email address over live chat.

## Changing your password while signed in

Go to **Account settings**, then **Security**, and choose **Change password**. You'll need to enter your current password. Changing your password signs you out on all other devices.

## Account lockout

To protect your account from people trying to guess your password, your account is locked for 15 minutes after 5 failed sign-in attempts in a row. During that time, you can't sign in, even with the correct password. Once the 15 minutes have passed, the lock is lifted automatically. Resetting your password also removes the lock straight away.

## Reset link problems

Password reset links expire after 30 minutes and can only be used once. If you select an old link, you'll see an "invalid or expired link" message; just request a new one. Only the most recent link you requested will work, so if you requested several emails, use the newest one.

## Suspicious activity

If you get a password reset email you didn't request, you can safely ignore it — your password won't change unless someone selects the link. If you think someone else has signed in to your account, reset your password immediately and turn on two-factor authentication.
