# Warranties and protection plans

## Our store-brand warranty

All store-brand products come with a 1-year limited warranty from the date of delivery. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. If a covered product fails, we'll repair it, replace it, or refund the purchase price, at our choice. Store-brand furniture and outdoor gear have an extended 3-year warranty on frames and structural parts.

The warranty doesn't cover normal wear and tear, cosmetic damage that doesn't affect how the product works, damage from misuse, improper assembly, or unauthorized repairs, or commercial use.

## Other brands

Products from other brands are covered by the manufacturer's own warranty. The warranty terms are shown on the product page and included in the box. For warranty claims on other brands, contact the manufacturer directly — they handle repairs and replacements, not us. Within the first 30 days, you can return defective items to us instead.

## Protection plans

For many electronics, appliances, and furniture items, you can add a protection plan at checkout, or within 30 days of delivery from **Order history**. Plans last 2 or 3 years from the date of delivery and are provided by our protection plan partner.

Protection plans cover everything the warranty covers, plus:

- Mechanical and electrical failures after the manufacturer's warranty ends.
- Power surges.
- Accidental damage from handling, such as drops, spills, and cracked screens, for eligible portable electronics.
- Accidental stains and rips for eligible furniture fabric.

Accidental damage isn't covered by the standard warranty, only by a protection plan. Plans don't cover loss, theft, or intentional damage.

## Making a claim

For store-brand warranty claims, contact us with your order number, a description of the problem, and photos or a video showing the fault. For protection plan claims, file the claim with the protection plan partner using the plan number in your confirmation email. Most claims are decided within 3 business days. If a repair is needed, you'll be sent a prepaid shipping box or a technician will be scheduled, depending on the item.

## Canceling a protection plan

You can cancel a protection plan within 30 days of buying it for a full refund. After 30 days, you receive a prorated refund, minus the cost of any claims paid. If you return the product itself, the protection plan is canceled and fully refunded automatically.
