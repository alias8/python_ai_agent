# Damaged or defective items

We're sorry if something arrived damaged or isn't working properly. We'll make it right with a replacement or a full refund, and you won't pay any return shipping.

## Report the problem within 7 days

Report damage or defects within 7 days of delivery so we can file a claim with the carrier while the details are fresh. Go to **Order history**, select the order, and choose **Report a problem**. You'll be asked for:

- A short description of what's wrong.
- Photos of the damaged item, and of the outer box and packaging.
- For items that don't work, a photo or short video showing the fault, if possible.

Most reports are reviewed within one business day. If we approve a replacement, we ship it straight away; you don't need to send the original item back first.

## Items that stop working later

If an item stops working after the 7-day window but within 30 days of delivery, you can still return it as defective through the normal returns process, with free return shipping and no restocking fee. After 30 days, defects are handled under the product's warranty — see our article on warranties and protection plans.

## What happens to the damaged item

For most items under $100, we won't ask you to return the damaged item; you can recycle or dispose of it. For more expensive items, we'll email you a prepaid return label so the item can be inspected or sent back to the manufacturer. Please return it within 14 days of receiving the label, or we may charge you for the replacement.

## Large or freight items

If a furniture or other freight delivery arrives damaged, note the damage on the delivery receipt before the driver leaves, even if you still accept the delivery. You can also refuse the delivery entirely if the damage is severe.

For freight items, keep all of the original packaging until your claim is closed. The freight carrier may send an inspector to look at the item and the packaging, and claims can be denied if the packaging has been thrown away. Don't attempt to repair or assemble a damaged freight item, as this can also affect the claim.

## Missing parts

If an item arrived with missing parts, hardware, or instructions, choose **Report a problem** and select **Missing parts**. We can usually send the missing parts within 3-5 business days, which is faster than replacing the whole item.
