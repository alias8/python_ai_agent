# Gift cards and promo codes

## Gift cards

Gift cards are available as digital cards, delivered by email, or physical cards, shipped for free. They come in amounts from $10 to $500 and never expire. There are no fees for buying or using them.

To use a gift card, enter the 16-digit card number and 4-digit PIN at checkout. If the gift card doesn't cover the whole order, you can pay the remaining balance with a card. You can check your balance at any time on the gift card page.

Gift cards can't be used to buy other gift cards or Plus memberships. Treat them like cash: we can't replace a gift card that's lost or stolen unless you have the original order confirmation for it.

## Promo codes

Enter promo codes in the **Promo code** box at checkout, not in the gift card box. Only one promo code can be used per order, and promo codes can't be combined with other offers unless the offer says so. Promo codes can't be applied to gift cards, Plus memberships, or items marked "Excluded from promotions."

If you forget to enter a promo code, we can't apply it after the order is placed.

## Promo code errors

- **E-6001: Promo code expired.** The promotion has ended. Promotions end at 11:59pm Eastern Time on their last day.
- **E-6002: Minimum not met.** Your order total, before tax and shipping, is below the promotion's minimum spend. Add items to reach the minimum.
- **E-6003: Not eligible for these items.** Nothing in your cart qualifies for this promotion, usually because the items are excluded.
- **E-6004: Code already used.** Single-use codes, such as welcome codes for new customers, can only be used once per account.

## Returns on orders with promo codes

If you return part of an order that used a promo code, your refund reflects the discount you actually received on that item. If returning items takes the order below a promotion's minimum spend, the discount on the remaining items is removed and the difference is deducted from your refund.

## Refunds on gift card purchases

If you paid for an order with a gift card, the refund goes back to the gift card, or to a new gift card if the original can't be found.

## Cashing out a gift card

Gift cards can't be exchanged for cash, except where required by law. In California, gift cards with a remaining balance under $10 can be redeemed for cash on request; contact us with the card number. Some other states have similar rules.
